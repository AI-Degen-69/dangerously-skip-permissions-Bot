### Microstructure-Calibrated Market-Making Execution Pipeline

1. **Station 1: LOB Microstructure Denoising ($\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$ — Savitzky–Golay Polynomial Filter)**
   $$\underbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{mid}}}(\color{#D19A66}{\tau})}_{\text{local polynomial fit}} = \sum_{k=0}^{\color{#C678DD}{d}} a_k \color{#D19A66}{\tau}^k, \quad \color{#D19A66}{\tau} \in [-\color{#D19A66}{m}, \color{#D19A66}{m}], \quad \mathcal{E} = \sum_{\color{#D19A66}{\tau}=-\color{#D19A66}{m}}^{\color{#D19A66}{m}} \left( \color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{mid}}}(\color{#D19A66}{\tau}) - \sum_{k=0}^{\color{#C678DD}{d}} a_k \color{#D19A66}{\tau}^k \right)^2$$
   $$\underbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}(t)}_{\text{denoised mid-price}} = \sum_{j=-\color{#D19A66}{m}}^{\color{#D19A66}{m}} \overbrace{\color{#E06C75}{c_j}}^{\text{SG weight}} \cdot \underbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{mid}}}(t + j)}_{\text{raw mid sample}}, \qquad \color{#E06C75}{c} = \frac{1}{35} \begin{bmatrix} -3 & 12 & 17 & 12 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} -0.085714 & 0.342857 & 0.485714 & 0.342857 & -0.085714 \end{bmatrix}$$
   - **Takes:** Discrete raw mid-price samples ($\color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{mid}}}$) at $\Delta t = 100\text{ ms}$, symmetric window half-width ($\color{#D19A66}{m} = 2$, length $2\color{#D19A66}{m}+1 = 5$), polynomial degree ($\color{#C678DD}{d} = 2$), pre-computed projection weights ($\color{#E06C75}{c_j}$).
   - **Calculates:** Smooth center-point ($\color{#D19A66}{\tau} = 0$) estimate of fair mid-price free from high-frequency microstructure noise.
   - **How:** Minimizes squared polynomial residuals $\mathcal{E}$ across the sliding window, reducing at $\color{#D19A66}{\tau} = 0$ to an exact linear convolution with $\color{#E06C75}{c_j}$.
   - **Why:** Removes micro-flickering quotes, rapid cancellations, and bid-ask bounces without the phase lag of moving averages, preventing excessive quote churn.
   - **Yields:** Denoised reference mid-price ($\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$).

2. **Station 2: Chainlink TWAP Resolution & Variance Decay ($\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2(t)}$)**
   $$\text{Resolution} = \begin{cases} \text{UP}, & \text{if } \overbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{\text{TWAP}}_{[\color{#D19A66}{T}-60\text{s}, \color{#D19A66}{T}]}}^{\text{final 60s window}} \ge \underbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{S}(t_0)}_{\text{inception spot}} \\ \text{DOWN}, & \text{if } \color{#E5C07B}{\text{TWAP}}_{[\color{#D19A66}{T}-60\text{s}, \color{#D19A66}{T}]} < \color{#E5C07B}{S}(t_0) \end{cases}, \qquad \underbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{\text{TWAP}}(t)}_{\text{running average}} = \frac{1}{\color{#D19A66}{t - t_{\text{start}}}} \int_{\color{#D19A66}{t_{\text{start}}}}^{\color{#D19A66}{t}} \color{#E5C07B}{S}(u) \, du$$
   $$\underbrace{\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}(t)}_{\text{effective variance}} = \overbrace{\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{spot}}^2}}^{\text{spot variance}} \cdot \underbrace{\left( \frac{\color{#D19A66}{T - t}}{\color{#D19A66}{T}} \right)^2}_{\text{quadratic time decay}}$$
   - **Takes:** Underlying spot stream ($\color{#E5C07B}{S}(u)$), inception reference spot ($\color{#E5C07B}{S}(t_0)$), annualized spot volatility ($\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{spot}}}$), elapsed time ($\color{#D19A66}{t}$), contract duration ($\color{#D19A66}{T}$), remaining fraction ($\color{#D19A66}{T - t}$).
   - **Calculates:** Running 60-second Time-Weighted Average Price ($\color{#E5C07B}{\text{TWAP}}(t)$) and residual settlement variance ($\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}(t)$).
   - **How:** Integrates spot price over elapsed time relative to $\color{#E5C07B}{S}(t_0)$ and scales $\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{spot}}^2}$ by the square of the remaining epoch fraction $\left(\frac{\color{#D19A66}{T - t}}{\color{#D19A66}{T}}\right)^2$.
   - **Why:** As $\color{#D19A66}{t} \to \color{#D19A66}{T}$, past spot prints become immutable in the terminal TWAP; quadratic decay prevents overreacting to late-stage spot spikes that cannot alter settlement.
   - **Yields:** Running settlement benchmark ($\color{#E5C07B}{\text{TWAP}}(t)$) and decayed effective variance ($\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}(t)$).

3. **Station 3: Order Flow Arrival Intensity ($\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}$)**
   $$\overbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{\lambda}(\color{#D19A66}{\delta})}^{\text{Poisson arrival rate}} = A e^{-\color{#E06C75}{\kappa} \color{#D19A66}{\delta}}, \qquad \underbrace{\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}}_{\text{liquidity depth}} = -\frac{\partial \ln \overbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{\Lambda}(\color{#D19A66}{\delta})}^{\text{cumulative depth}}}{\partial \underbrace{\color{#D19A66}{\delta}}_{\text{mid distance}}} \approx \frac{\ln(\color{#61AFEF}{V_1}) - \ln(\color{#61AFEF}{V_k})}{\color{#D19A66}{\delta_k} - \color{#D19A66}{\delta_1}}$$
   - **Takes:** Distance from mid-price ($\color{#D19A66}{\delta_1}, \color{#D19A66}{\delta_k}$), Poisson arrival rate ($\color{#61AFEF}{\lambda}(\color{#D19A66}{\delta})$), cumulative resting L2 book volumes ($\color{#61AFEF}{\Lambda}(\color{#D19A66}{\delta})$ via $\color{#61AFEF}{V_1}, \color{#61AFEF}{V_k}$).
   - **Calculates:** Real-time liquidity decay elasticity measuring how fast fill probability drops as quotes move away from mid-price.
   - **How:** Estimates the negative logarithmic volume gradient across cumulative Level-2 price levels.
   - **Why:** High $\color{#E06C75}{\kappa} \to \infty$ signals a thick, resilient book where spreads contract; low $\color{#E06C75}{\kappa} \to 0$ signals a fragile, thin book requiring wider spreads against adverse flow.
   - **Yields:** Dynamic book depth elasticity parameter ($\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}$).

4. **Station 4: Optimal Spread Derivation ($\color{#98C379}{s^*}$)**
   $$U(W) = -e^{-\color{#C678DD}{\gamma} W} \implies \underbrace{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}_{\text{optimal full spread}} = \overbrace{\frac{2}{\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}}}^{\text{risk scaling}} \ln\left( 1 + \underbrace{\frac{\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}}{\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}}}_{\text{risk-to-depth ratio}} \right), \qquad \underbrace{\color{#98C379}{\delta_{\text{half}}}}_{\text{half-spread}} = \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2} = \frac{1}{\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}} \ln\left( 1 + \frac{\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}}{\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}} \right)$$
   - **Takes:** Liquidity depth elasticity ($\color{#E06C75}{\kappa}$), CARA absolute risk aversion coefficient ($\color{#C678DD}{\gamma} > 0$).
   - **Calculates:** Optimal end-to-end full spread width ($\color{#98C379}{s^*}$) and symmetric half-spread ($\color{#98C379}{\delta_{\text{half}}}$), completely independent of current inventory ($\color{#61AFEF}{q}$).
   - **How:** Solves the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) optimal control equation under CARA utility $U(W) = -e^{-\color{#C678DD}{\gamma} W}$ and splits $\color{#98C379}{s^*}$ evenly into halves.
   - **Why:** Guarantees enough edge per round-trip pair to compensate for execution risk and volatility, while keeping inventory bias isolated to the quote center.
   - **Yields:** Optimal full spread ($\color{#98C379}{s^*}$) and symmetric half-spread ($\color{#98C379}{\delta_{\text{half}}}$).

5. **Station 5: Reservation Price & Inventory Skew ($\color{#56B6C2}{r_i}$)**
   $$\underbrace{\color{#56B6C2}{r_i}(\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}, \color{#61AFEF}{q}, t)}_{\text{reservation price}} = \overbrace{\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}}^{\text{denoised mid}} - \underbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{q}}_{\text{net inventory}} \cdot \overbrace{\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}}^{\text{risk aversion}} \underbrace{\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}}_{\text{decayed variance}} \overbrace{(\color{#D19A66}{T - t})}^{\text{time remaining}}$$
   - **Takes:** Denoised mid-price ($\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$), net unhedged contract inventory ($\color{#61AFEF}{q}$, positive for excess UP, negative for excess DOWN), risk aversion ($\color{#C678DD}{\gamma}$), decayed variance ($\color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}$), remaining time ($\color{#D19A66}{T - t}$).
   - **Calculates:** Subjective indifference valuation where holding current inventory equals executing an incremental contract.
   - **How:** Shifts $\color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$ linearly by $\color{#61AFEF}{q} \cdot \color{#C678DD}{\gamma} \color{#E06C75}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}^2}(\color{#D19A66}{T - t})$: neutral when $\color{#61AFEF}{q} = 0$ ($\color{#56B6C2}{r_i} = \color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$), lower when long UP ($\color{#61AFEF}{q} > 0 \implies \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} < \color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$), higher when long DOWN ($\color{#61AFEF}{q} < 0 \implies \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} > \color{#E5C07B}{s_i}$).
   - **Why:** Repels further accumulation of the heavy side and pulls the complementary quote closer to market to accelerate inventory balancing.
   - **Yields:** Inventory-skewed reservation price ($\color{#56B6C2}{r_i}$).

6. **Station 6: Dual-Bid Quoting Matrix ($\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{UP}}}, \color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{DN}}}$ — "Everything is a Bid")**
   $$\underbrace{\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{UP}}}}_{\text{target UP bid}} = \overbrace{\color{#56B6C2}{r_i}}^{\text{fair center}} - \underbrace{\frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2}}_{\text{half-spread}}, \qquad \overbrace{\color{#E06C75}{p_{\text{ask}}^{\text{UP}}}}^{\text{synthetic UP ask}} = \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} + \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2} \iff \underbrace{\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{DN}}}}_{\text{target DOWN bid}} = 1.00 - \overbrace{\color{#E06C75}{p_{\text{ask}}^{\text{UP}}}}^{\text{CTF parity}} = 1.00 - \left( \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} + \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2} \right)$$
   $$\underbrace{\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{UP}}} + \color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{DN}}}}_{\text{combined pair cost}} = \left( \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} - \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2} \right) + \left( 1.00 - \color{#56B6C2}{r_i} - \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2} \right) = 1.00 - \color{#98C379}{s^*} < 1.00\text{ USDC}$$
   - **Takes:** Reservation price ($\color{#56B6C2}{r_i}$), optimal full spread ($\color{#98C379}{s^*}$), binary CTF parity invariant ($\text{Payoff}(\text{UP}) + \text{Payoff}(\text{DOWN}) \equiv 1.00\text{ USDC}$).
   - **Calculates:** Target passive bid prices for both UP and DOWN outcome tokens without requiring pre-existing sell inventory.
   - **How:** Quotes the UP bid directly at $\color{#56B6C2}{r_i} - \frac{\color{#98C379}{s^*}}{2}$ and converts the theoretical UP ask ($\color{#E06C75}{p_{\text{ask}}^{\text{UP}}}$) into an equivalent complementary DOWN bid via $1.00 - \color{#E06C75}{p_{\text{ask}}^{\text{UP}}}$.
   - **Why:** Guarantees algebraically that paired acquisition clears strictly below \$1.00 by exact gross margin $\color{#98C379}{s^*}$ (e.g., $\color{#98C379}{s^*} = 0.04 \implies \text{pair cost} = 0.96$) for all finite $\color{#E06C75}{\kappa} > 0$ and $\color{#C678DD}{\gamma} > 0$.
   - **Yields:** Dual target bid matrix ($\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{UP}}}$, $\color{#98C379}{p_{\text{bid}}^{\text{DN}}}$).

7. **Station 7: Absolute Dollar-Notional Risk Management ($\color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}}, \color{#61AFEF}{Q_{\max}(\color{#D19A66}{p})}$)**
   $$\underbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{Q_{\max}}(\color{#D19A66}{p})}_{\text{max leg shares}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\overbrace{\color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}}}^{\text{dollar cap}}}{\underbrace{\color{#D19A66}{p}}_{\text{contract price}}} \right\rfloor, \qquad \underbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{bid}}}(\color{#D19A66}{p})}_{\text{active quote size}} = \color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{base}}} \times \max\left( 0, \; 1 - \frac{\overbrace{\color{#61AFEF}{q_{\text{leg}}} \cdot \color{#D19A66}{p}}^{\text{committed USD}}}{\underbrace{\color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}}}_{\text{dollar cap}}} \right)$$
   - **Takes:** Maximum single-leg unhedged dollar boundary ($\color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}}$), contract bid price ($\color{#D19A66}{p}$), current leg inventory ($\color{#61AFEF}{q_{\text{leg}}}$), baseline order size ($\color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{base}}}$).
   - **Calculates:** Inverse price-scaled share cap ($\color{#61AFEF}{Q_{\max}}(\color{#D19A66}{p})$) and linearly throttled bid quantity ($\color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{bid}}}(\color{#D19A66}{p})$).
   - **How:** Caps shares inversely with price (under $\color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}} = \$50.00$, $250$ shares at $\$0.20$ vs. $83$ shares at $\$0.60$) and scales $\color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{base}}}$ linearly toward zero as committed dollar exposure $\color{#61AFEF}{q_{\text{leg}}} \cdot \color{#D19A66}{p} \to \color{#D19A66}{V_{\max}}$.
   - **Why:** Eliminates static-share risk asymmetry (100 contracts at $\$0.60$ commit $\$60.00$ versus $\$20.00$ at $\$0.20$), suppresses toxic accumulation on a filled leg, and directs capital to the unfilled leg to complete pairs.
   - **Yields:** Risk-bounded maximum share capacity ($\color{#61AFEF}{Q_{\max}}$) and active leg quote size ($\color{#61AFEF}{\text{Size}_{\text{bid}}}$).

8. **Station 8: Queue Preservation & Hysteresis Gate ($\color{#C678DD}{\epsilon}$)**
   $$\text{Decision} = \begin{cases} \text{HOLD}, & \text{if } \underbrace{\Delta = |\color{#61AFEF}{p_{\text{target}}} - \color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{active}}}|}_{\text{absolute price drift}} < \overbrace{\color{#C678DD}{\epsilon}}^{\text{tick deadband } (0.01)} \\ \text{CANCEL\_AND\_REPLACE}, & \text{if } \underbrace{\Delta = |\color{#61AFEF}{p_{\text{target}}} - \color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{active}}}|}_{\text{absolute price drift}} \ge \overbrace{\color{#C678DD}{\epsilon}}^{\text{tick deadband } (0.01)} \end{cases}, \qquad \color{#C678DD}{\epsilon} = 0.01\text{ USDC}$$
   - **Takes:** Newly computed target bid ($\color{#61AFEF}{p_{\text{target}}}$), active resting order price ($\color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{active}}}$), market tick hysteresis deadband ($\color{#C678DD}{\epsilon} = 0.01\text{ USDC}$).
   - **Calculates:** Discrete order-update decision separating sub-tick model micro-drift ($\Delta < 0.01$) from full-tick market shifts ($\Delta \ge 0.01$).
   - **How:** Compares absolute price displacement $\Delta = |\color{#61AFEF}{p_{\text{target}}} - \color{#E5C07B}{p_{\text{active}}}|$ against the 1-cent tick deadband $\color{#C678DD}{\epsilon}$.
   - **Why:** Preserves First-In, First-Out (FIFO) queue seniority during continuous sub-tick re-evaluation while triggering immediate cancel-replace on full-tick spot moves to avoid adverse selection.
   - **Yields:** Discrete execution decision (`HOLD` vs. `CANCEL_AND_REPLACE`).
