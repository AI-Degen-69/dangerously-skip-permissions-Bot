Wallet forensics·three-week extension·2026-11-12

Three weeks confirm the machine.
They do not confirm the edge.

Extending the one-week sample to 21 days keeps every structural finding — the two-sided quoting, the locked spread capture, the ~T−10 s cutoff, the daily 50% fee rebate — and overturns two economic conclusions. Over three weeks the wallet made +$66,190, but 81% of it was rebate, and the trading gain it looked like we had found came almost entirely from a single week.

Where the $66,190 actually comes from

Locked spread capture is 89.4% of gross trading profit. Fees take back almost exactly what trading earns; the rebate returns half of them.

  • Trading is not flat, but it is not reliably profitable either.Over 21 days net trading is +27.9 bps. Split by week it is −7.2, +99.6, +2.6 bps — the entire gain is one week. Report it as a range, not a rate.
  • The 50% rebate rule is the only invariant.Rebate paid on day D equals exactly half of day D−1's taker fees — to the cent on 15 of 20 day-pairs, within 0.5% on the rest. It held every single day.
  • The residual directional book is noise, not a leak.The week showed −55 bps. Three weeks show +26.5 bps at t = 1.43. There is nothing there to fix, in either direction.
  • Variance is enormous relative to the trading edge.Per-market net has σ = $111.77 against a mean of $2.06, and market-level drawdown reached −$5,865. The rebate is what makes the equity curve look calm.

The mechanism in one paragraph

It quotes both sides of a 5-minute BTC Up/Down slot from the first seconds after open, buys 15–30 shares at a time, and never sells. Because it holds both tokens of the same market to settlement, every matched pair it owns pays out exactly one dollar. If it bought the pair for less than $1, the difference is locked — no forecast involved. The price of that certainty is that it pays taker fees on 76% of its cash and has to carry whatever imbalance it accumulated.

09

Method and what this cannot answer

The pull script accepts only 1- or 7-day windows, so the sample is three tiled 7-day pulls merged on identical rows.

  • Integrity486,244 rows after dropping 167 boundary duplicates; 475,807 trades, 10,395 redemptions, 42 daily rebate rows. Zero fills outside the market window.
  • Coverage 92.4%5,589 of ~6,047 slots. Gaps concentrate on Oct 30 (180 markets), Nov 1 (153) and Oct 21 (234).
  • AnchoredThe third pull reproduces the committed one-week file exactly — 174,019 rows, 72,744,461 bytes.
  • Invisible to this dataUnfilled quotes, queue position, intra-window mark-to-market, adverse selection beyond the next same-token fill, redemption gas.
  • Inferred, not observedTaker/maker labels come from the fee, not from exchange order data. The maker reward has no formula that fits; it is ~$1/market/day.
  • Sample realism21 days is one regime. It contains three weekends but only one of them is like the next.
